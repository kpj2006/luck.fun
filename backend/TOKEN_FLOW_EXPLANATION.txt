RUGS.FUN - TOKEN FLOW EXPLANATION
===================================

This document explains exactly how RUGS tokens move between wallets and contracts
during a live game cycle.

1. THE DEPOSIT (Actual Transfer - Wallet to Contract)
---------------------------------------------------
When a player starts, they must deposit RUGS into the platform.
- Action: User wallet sends ERC20 tokens to the 'RugsFun' contract address.
- Result: User's wallet balance decreases; RugsFun contract balance increases.
- Internal: The contract updates its mapping: balances[user] = amount.

2. THE GAME PLAY (Internal Accounting - No Gas Fees)
--------------------------------------------------
During the game (Buy/Sell/Crash), tokens do NOT move between wallets. This
ensures the game is fast and doesn't require gas for every single tick.
- Buy: The 'RugsFun' contract decrements the user's internal balance number.
- Win: The contract increments the user's internal balance number.
- Loss: No action needed (the balance was already reduced during Buy).

3. THE SETTLEMENT (Actual Transfer - Pool to Treasury)
-----------------------------------------------------
When the platform takes its cut (Fees or Lost Bets), the tokens ACTUALLY move.
- Action: The 'RugsFun' contract calls 'safeTransfer' to move tokens from
  its pool into the 'Treasury' contract address.
- Result: This is the "Platform Profit." These are real ERC20 tokens locked
  in the Treasury vault.

4. THE WITHDRAWAL (Actual Transfer - Contract to Wallet)
------------------------------------------------------
When a player wants their winnings back in their personal wallet.
- Action: User calls 'withdraw'.
- Result: The contract sends the physical ERC20 tokens from the contract
  address back to the user's personal wallet address.


SUMMARY TABLE
=============

| Action            | Actual Tokens Move? | Source ➔ Destination          |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|
| Deposit           | YES                 | User Wallet ➔ Contract Pool   |
| Play / Win / Loss | NO (Internal)       | Mapping Credit ➔ Mapping Debit |
| Platform Fees     | YES                 | Contract Pool ➔ Treasury      |
| Withdrawal        | YES                 | Contract Pool ➔ User Wallet   |
